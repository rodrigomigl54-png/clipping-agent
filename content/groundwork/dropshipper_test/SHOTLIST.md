> You watch it twice. Then you watch his next one.
> By midnight you've learned three new words. Dropshipping. Winning product. Scale.
> You don't fully understand any of them, but you understand the screenshot of his sales dashboard, and that's enough.
> You sign up for a store builder that night. The free trial ends in two weeks, and the monthly fee after that feels like nothing next to forty thousand dollars.
> You spend the entire weekend building a store for a galaxy projector you found online for six dollars and eighty cents.
> You price it at thirty-four ninety-nine. You do the math eleven times, and it keeps coming out beautiful.
> You buy a logo for five dollars. You tell your roommate you're working on something, and you like the way it sounds.
> Then, on a Thursday, at two fourteen in the afternoon, your phone buzzes in the middle of a lecture.
> Your first sale.
> Thirty-four ninety-nine, from a woman in Ohio you will never meet.
> You don't hear another word for the rest of the class. You've already spent the profit four different ways in your head.
> You don't know yet that the ad that found her cost you forty-one dollars. You don't know the projector will take nineteen days to arrive.
> For now, you're a business owner. And it feels incredible.
